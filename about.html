Our platform traces Rwanda’s journey from tragedy to triumph, celebrating the resilience of its people, the vision of its leaders, its technological progress, and its commitment to sustainability.

Through audio, video, and written word, we bring these stories to life for audiences everywhere.

Accessibility Features

StoryCast is built to be usable by everyone. We follow WCAG 2.1 AA standards.

Captions: every video includes captions for viewers who are deaf or hard of hearing.

Transcripts: every audio and video story comes with a full written transcript.

Keyboard navigation: the whole site can be used without a mouse, with clearly visible focus outlines.

Screen-reader support: semantic structure, descriptive image text, and ARIA labels where needed.

Readable design: high-contrast colors and clear, resizable text.

How to Engage with Our Stories

Watch: play a video story with captions on or off.

Listen: choose the audio-only version when you’re on the go.

Read: follow along with the full transcript at your own pace.

Explore: browse related stories at the bottom of each story page.
